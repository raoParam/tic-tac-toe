How to play

RULEBOOK / 01
  1. 01

    Take your turn

    O goes first, then X. Choose any empty square.

  2. 02

    Make a line

    Match three marks horizontally, vertically or diagonally.

  3. 03

    Win the round

    The first player to complete a line wins one point.

  4. 04

    Share a draw

    If the board fills with no winner, both players earn 0.5 points.

Remember: Restart keeps the scores. Reset All starts the match over.
